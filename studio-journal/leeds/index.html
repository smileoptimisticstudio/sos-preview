2 October 2026 · Leeds · Conference talk

The Hospital Joy Toolkit at SoHPS Northern Study Day

The official CPD programme lists “The Hospital Joy Toolkit” presented by Yuliia Barsukova, Smile Optimistic Studio CIC, in the late-morning presentation session.

I came to share the Hospital Joy Toolkit, but left with a deeper respect for the knowledge, creativity and generosity of Health Play Specialists. Their professional discussions reinforced the importance of giving children time, choice and emotional support around healthcare experiences.

The day also prompted questions about how new resources might fit alongside existing health play practice, and how careful evaluation and practitioner feedback could inform their use.
