26 June 2026 · London · Poster presentation

Cards that help a child stay with a feeling — not just away from it

Smile Optimistic Studio CIC presented a poster at the Society of Health Play Specialists' 3rd Annual Conference, held at UCLH in London under the theme “Children at the Heart of Healthcare”.

The poster introduced the creative methodology behind the Hospital Joy Toolkit: a resource shaped by six years of family experience during long-term childhood cancer treatment, with a focus on emotional expression, connection and a child's freedom to choose.

The conference poster

Professional contributions

Health Play Specialists have contributed professional knowledge and experience that informed the development of the cards. Their contribution is acknowledged in the Toolkit’s Practice & Training Guide, as requested.
